The work behind the numbers

Rolling out GK OmniPOS across 70 stores, twice over

SituationA footwear retailer replacing its legacy till estate while simultaneously implementing SAP S/4HANA and SAP CAR at head office. Two store networks with different owners, different fiscal entities and different operating rules.
ActionI was the functional lead on the client side from November 2018. I defined the target store processes, stayed through the whole build cycle, learned the platform hands-on alongside the implementing consultant — servers, configuration, extensions — and then trained and supported the stores myself.

6-month programme · Platform upgrade

GK 5.11 → 5.21 across 70 live stores, without closing a single till

SituationA full-version jump requiring complete reinstallation and reconfiguration — and a move from Windows Server to Linux — on an estate that was trading every day.
ActionRan both platforms in parallel with DEV, QAS and PRD environments on each version, and built a physical device lab where we walked the full scenario matrix before any store was touched. Eight custom extensions had to be rebuilt and revalidated along the way.
Result70 stores migrated with zero downtime. Around 100 incidents a month across the estate, resolved without interrupting trade.

8 extensions · Custom development

Making GK fit Mexican footwear retail

SituationFootwear sells in a size grid, wholesale tickets behave differently from consumer tickets, and Mexican tax law requires an electronic invoice at the moment of sale.
ActionStood up a dedicated environment for GK extension development and delivered eight of them — including a size-matrix selling screen that made wholesale ticketing possible at the till, REST API integration of the Tarjeta Amiga payment method, API integration of corporate voucher schemes, and CFDI 4.0 self-invoicing.
ResultThe platform absorbed the local business model instead of the business bending around the platform — the difference between a rollout that sticks and one that gets rolled back.
